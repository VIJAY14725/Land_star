Marketing description

Approximately 291.62 acres on FM 2268 near Holland offer room to evaluate a country estate, agricultural operation, equestrian use or a phased residential land-development concept. Open hay fields, mature trees and rolling terrain create a setting for continued rural use as well as longer-term planning.

The property has FM 2268 frontage and regional access toward State Highway 95, Holland and Bartlett. Seller materials identify Oncor electric service and a 3-inch Bell-Milam-Falls Water Supply Corporation line along the road frontage. Line presence does not establish sufficient capacity for a subdivision; service availability, improvements, connection costs and timing require provider confirmation.

Residential lot sizes and yield should be determined through engineering, market analysis and review of water, wastewater, drainage, floodplain, access and applicable subdivision requirements. Any concept plan is preliminary; its current submission and approval status must be verified. No approved subdivision, guaranteed density or utility capacity is represented.

Contact LAND-STAR Real Estate to request the survey, preliminary concept plan and available property materials, or to arrange a property tour.
